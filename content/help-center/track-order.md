You can track your order from Account → Orders, or with the link in your shipping confirmation email. Tracking usually updates within 24 hours of shipping.

## Details

Customers can track orders from Account → Orders or via the link in the shipping confirmation email. Tracking usually updates within 24 hours of the label being created.
