You can change the shipping address from Account → Orders as long as the order hasn’t shipped yet (usually within 2 hours of purchase). After it ships, we can ask the carrier to redirect it.

## Details

Addresses can be edited from Account → Orders until the order ships (usually within 2 hours of purchase). After shipping, request a carrier redirect.
