I’m sorry it arrived damaged. Reply with a photo of the item and the packaging and we’ll send a free replacement or a full refund — you don’t need to send the damaged item back.

## Details

For items that arrive damaged, request a photo of the item and packaging, then offer a free replacement or full refund. The customer does not need to return the damaged item.
