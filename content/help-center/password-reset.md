Head to the sign-in page and choose “Forgot password”. We’ll email a reset link that’s valid for 30 minutes — if it doesn’t show up, check your spam folder.

## Details

Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in page to receive a reset link, valid for 30 minutes. If the email doesn’t arrive, check spam, then verify the email address on the account.
