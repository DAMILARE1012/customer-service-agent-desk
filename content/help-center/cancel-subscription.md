You can cancel any time from Account → Subscription → Cancel plan. You’ll keep access until the end of your current billing period and won’t be charged again.

## Details

Subscriptions can be cancelled from Account → Subscription → Cancel plan. Access continues until the end of the current billing period; no further charges are made.
