Sorry about that! Packages marked delivered sometimes turn up within 48 hours — it’s worth checking with neighbours and around your door. If it still hasn’t arrived after 48 hours, we’ll open a carrier investigation and send a replacement.

## Details

Carriers sometimes mark packages delivered up to 48 hours early. Ask the customer to check with neighbours and around the property. After 48 hours, open a carrier investigation and ship a replacement at no cost.
